Political Hexagon Test

I have created this simple political test as an alternative to the popular Political Compass test.

>>> TAKE THE TEST <<<

I created this test to address some oversights I find in other political tests online. One, the original test, while elegant and simple, does not really reflect actual ideologies well in its results. It tends to skew towards the bottom-left quadrant. Other tests I find are very long-winded and specific and don't entirely map directly onto one ideology.

I originally wanted to make a triangular compass based on the Hearts of Iron III political alignment chart (a bit of an esoteric inspiration, but I digress) but I found that unless I expanded it into this hexagon it did not properly map out every real-world ideology.

I also wanted to make sure certain fringe ideologies were equally represented to more common ones, because it is my personal belief that we are biased to consider certain viewpoints as more valid simply because we are more used to them. This test is definitely biased but please ignore that.
